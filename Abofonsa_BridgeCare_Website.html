The process

Getting started takes about a week

A straightforward assessment, a written plan, then care begins. No lengthy contracts and no obligation to continue beyond your notice period.

1

Consultation

We talk through the situation by phone or at home — conditions, routines, and what the family needs most.

2

Clinical assessment

A registered nurse visits, reviews medical history and medication, and documents a baseline.

3

Your care plan

You receive a written daily service plan with visit times, named professionals and agreed costs.

4

Care begins

Visits start on schedule. You receive a report after each one and can adjust the plan at any time.

Our approach

Care that is coordinated, not improvised

Traditional care asks patients to navigate rigid schedules, travel barriers and medical records scattered across facilities. We move the coordination burden onto our team instead.

Scheduling that holds

If a professional is unavailable, cover is arranged and reassigned before the visit is missed — which is how we sustain 99% shift fulfilment.

Monitoring between visits

Connected devices record vital signs continuously. Readings outside the boundaries your clinician sets raise an alert rather than waiting for the next visit.

Escalation that matches severity

Minor changes notify your nominated family contact, moderate concerns alert the assigned nurse, and critical events dispatch emergency services directly.

The Angel network

Someone is always accountable

Every patient has a named Angel — a nominated relative or trusted proxy who receives visit reports and alerts on the patient's behalf. It matters most when family live abroad, or when a patient would rather not manage appointments and medication alone.

A report after every visit

Vital signs, tasks completed, medication administered and the carer's own notes — countersigned by the supervising nurse.

A direct line to the care team

Message the assigned nurse or call the coordination desk without going through a switchboard.
